Story:
I moved to the U.S. when I was 19, unsure what computer science meant to me beyond liking computers. It started to click when I saw it as system design, not just code. I liked how messy real-world problems could be modeled, broken down, connected, tested, and improved. Sometimes the solution is elegant. Sometimes it is not, but it still has to work because people depend on it.

That has shaped the work I have chased. At AAIS, I automated a weekly SQL billing process with Python, PySpark, and AWS Glue over 20+ TB of insurance data, helped migrate 160+ tables into 25 MDM domains, and built automation that generated 1,000+ production SQL tables. More recently, I have worked on applied AI systems, including a LiteLLM AI Gateway with smart-router tiers, guardrails, traces, alerts, CI gates, and 44 red-team fixtures. I also built an ICSME-published LLM pipeline that turns commits, diffs, linked issues, and metadata into grounded PR descriptions across 1,450 PRs.

I do not want to be someone who only writes code from a ticket. I want to become a seasoned engineer who understands systems deeply, makes good tradeoffs, communicates clearly, and builds things that hold up under real use.
