# Referral Outreach Template

Use this for LinkedIn DMs, email introductions, referral asks, recruiter replies, and coffee-chat follow-ups.

## Rules

- Match the channel. LinkedIn DMs should usually be 2-5 short sentences.
- Initial outreach may include one compact reason for fit, one concrete proof point, and Aryan's website or resume link.
- Follow-ups should be shorter than the first message.
- Do not repeat Aryan's work history, tech stack, publications, or project list in a follow-up unless the recipient asked for more detail.
- Avoid sounding desperate. Do not use phrases like `I'd really appreciate the chance`, `please consider me`, or `if you think my background is good enough`.
- Keep the ask simple: a brief chat, coffee chat, or whether they are open to connecting.
- Use plain language and no em dashes or semicolons.

## Follow-Up Shape

```text
Hi <Name>, just following up here. Would be glad to chat briefly about <company/team/role> if you're open to it.
```

## Slightly Warmer Coffee-Chat Shape

```text
Hi <Name>, just following up here. Would be glad to hear more about <company/team/role> if you're open to a quick coffee chat.
```
