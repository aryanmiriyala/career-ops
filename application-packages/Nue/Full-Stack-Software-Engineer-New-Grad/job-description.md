## About Nue.io

Nue is the AI-powered revenue architecture platform that unifies CPQ, billing, and revenue lifecycle management into a single system. It enables companies to simplify complex pricing, automate monetization, and gain control over how they generate and scale revenue.

The pasted posting says Nue works with customers such as OpenAI, Chilipiper, Glean, HootSuite, and Mews, helping sales, operations, and finance teams gain revenue acceleration, operational efficiency, and revenue controls at speed and scale.

## What You Will Do

Nue's new grad full-stack engineers join a small, senior, distributed product and engineering team and work on production systems from day one. Engineers are expected to be product- and customer-focused, working with product, design, and customer-facing teams to understand real customer workflows, define success metrics, and iterate quickly.

Responsibilities:

- Build and ship full-stack features end-to-end, from APIs and data models to UI components and tests, in core product teams such as AI, Billing and Collections, Data and Integrations, or CPQ and Lifecycle Manager.
- Develop and extend backend services, primarily Java/Spring Boot or similar, including REST/GraphQL APIs, data access layers, and event-driven components with a focus on transactional correctness, performance, and scalability.
- Work on frontend applications using modern TypeScript/JavaScript frameworks to deliver intuitive workflows for finance and revenue teams.
- Help build and maintain integrations with systems such as NetSuite, Sage Intacct, QuickBooks, Microsoft Dynamics, Salesforce, Stripe, tax systems, GL systems, APIs, webhooks, and iPaaS tools such as Tray.io and Salesforce Flows.
- Write clean, test-driven code and contribute to automated testing, CI/CD pipelines, and monitoring to keep systems reliable in production.
- Use AI-assisted coding tools such as Claude Code and ChatGPT while maintaining engineering judgment and code quality.
- Collaborate with senior engineers, product managers, and customer-facing teams to understand customer problems and iterate quickly.
- Participate in code reviews, design discussions, and technical deep dives on distributed systems, performance optimization, and data modeling.

## What You Will Bring

- Pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field.
- Solid programming experience in at least one of Java, TypeScript/JavaScript, or Python, with exposure to building web applications.
- Strong understanding of core CS fundamentals: data structures, algorithms, concurrency basics, and database concepts.
- Experience building non-trivial applications through internships, projects, or co-ops, ideally including APIs, services, or full-stack features.
- Familiarity with Git, basic Linux/CLI workflows, and at least one cloud provider or containerization concept such as Docker or AWS.
- Strong communication and collaboration skills, including asking thoughtful questions, proactively raising issues, and working in a remote, cross-functional team.
- Demonstrated curiosity, ownership, and learning velocity around new frameworks, cloud services, and AI tooling.
