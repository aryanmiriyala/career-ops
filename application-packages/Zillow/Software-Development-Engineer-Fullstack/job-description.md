## About the team

You'll join a collaborative software engineering team building end-to-end web experiences that help customers and internal partners move faster and make confident decisions. Our team works across front-end and back-end systems, partnering closely with product, design, and other engineers to deliver scalable, reliable, and high-quality solutions. We value thoughtful technical judgment, continuous improvement, and a supportive team culture where engineers can grow while making meaningful impact.

## About the role

The Specialist II, Software Engineering, Full Stack designs, builds, and maintains end-to-end web applications across front-end and back-end layers, enabling scalable, reliable, and high-quality experiences for Zillow customers and internal users. Full stack engineers at this level apply seasoned technical judgment, collaborate closely with product and cross-functional partners, and contribute to code quality, operational excellence, and incremental improvements to system design and team practices.

### You will get to

- Design, develop, and maintain full stack features and services, implementing user interfaces and APIs that are performant, secure, and easy to operate in production.
- Collaborate with product, design, data, and peer engineering teams to refine requirements, break down work, and deliver iterative improvements that enhance customer and business outcomes.
- Write clean, testable, and maintainable code; create and maintain automated tests; and participate in code reviews to uphold engineering standards and best practices.
- Monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize applications and services in production, using logs, metrics, and alerts to improve reliability, performance, and developer experience.
- Contribute to technical design discussions, documentation, and implementation plans for moderately complex features and systems, providing options and trade-off analysis.
- Mentor and support peers through pairing, knowledge sharing, and constructive feedback, helping to elevate team capabilities and inclusive engineering culture.
