# Faraday Full Stack Engineer, New Grad Application Questions

## Why Faraday?

Question: Tell us why you'd like to work here

Answer:

I’d like to work at Faraday because the problem feels real in a way that matters to me. Permits, warranty claims, rebates, and contractor paperwork are not abstract software problems. They are messy workflows that take time away from people who are trying to get actual work done, and I like building tools that make that kind of process clearer and faster.

That connects closely to the work I’ve enjoyed most. At SmartSolve, I built internal workflow tooling with Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Drizzle ORM, SSO, and auth middleware around sensitive employee data. I’ve also built AI-heavy projects like Fix-It-Flow, where the hard part was connecting the user flow, backend state, model calls, and structured output into something useful instead of just calling an LLM.

I’m also excited by how much ownership this role seems to offer. I want to be close to users, ship quickly, learn from senior engineers in person, and keep improving as an engineer by working on product surfaces that people actually depend on.
