About the job
Job description
About the Opportunity: Are you passionate about the power of emerging technology to revolutionize public safety? Do you thrive on building complex, secure systems that solve high-stakes, real-world problems? If so, we invite you to join our team as a Full-Stack Engineer to help scale Flash AI, a cutting-edge platform that integrates video, audio, and document pipelines within a secure, CJIS-compliant infrastructure deployed on AWS GovCloud environment.

In this role, you will help build and scale both the backend services and front-end interfaces of our platform, leveraging microservices, LLMs, and RAG to empower officers to search through massive amounts of data using natural language. This opportunity is ideal for an adaptable, curious developer excited to work across the stack, from designing APIs to crafting Angular-based user interfaces, while continuously mastering new tools. If you’re ready to engineer the future of digital forensics and build scalable tech that makes a tangible impact on community safety, we’d love to hear from you.

Responsibilities
Design, build, and maintain backend microservices using FastAPI (Python).
Develop and maintain Angular front-end applications used by investigators and analysts.
Design and manage databases (PostgreSQL, Neo4j) for structured evidence data.
Build secure, scalable APIs consumed by Angular/React front-ends.
Collaborate across the stack to deliver features from concept to production.
Help optimize systems for large-scale evidence ingestion, processing, and search.

Skills and Experience:
Strong foundational programming skills with Python or a similar backend language, complemented by experience working with relational databases like PostgreSQL.
A proven ability to develop modern web applications using Angular or comparable front-end frameworks, experience working with Docker and cloud platforms (AWS preferred).
Proficiency in using agentic programming tools such as Cursor, Codex, or Claude Code.
A generalist mindset and a genuine curiosity for emerging technologies like LLMs, RAG, and vector databases (Weaviate, Pinecone); while the technology experience is a plus, we value the ability to learn new tools quickly.
Knowledge of AI/ML model deployment (SageMaker, Bedrock)
Prior work in high security or CJIS-Compliant environments.
Exceptional communication and interpersonal skills, with a strong sense of ownership over features and the adaptability to thrive in a fast-moving startup environment.
Demonstrated organization and attention to detail, ensuring reliability whether you are deploying microservices or designing user-facing interfaces.
Adaptability to an evolving technology stack; we do not expect candidates to have mastery of every tool listed, but we do look for a passion for learning and the ability to grow alongside our platform.

Why work with us
If you’re ready to make a meaningful impact by empowering law enforcement with intelligent automation to uncover truth faster, analyze evidence deeper, and deliver justice with precision, we invite you to apply.

Come be part of a team on a mission to revolutionize the way law enforcement analyzes evidence. We are committed to creating a lasting, real-world impact for every officer and agency we serve by providing the tools they need to navigate complex digital landscapes with confidence.

At Flash AI, our people come first. Our Unlimited PTO policy gives employees the flexibility to take the time they need, trusting that true balance and well-being lead to your best work.
