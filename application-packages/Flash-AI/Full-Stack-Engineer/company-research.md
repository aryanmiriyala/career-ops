# Company Research - Flash AI

Research date: 2026-08-26

Sources:

- Flash AI Security & Trust page: https://www.flashai.us/security
- Flash AI Security Whitepaper: https://www.flashai.us/security/whitepaper

Notes:

- Flash AI presents the product as investigative intelligence software for law enforcement, with Flash PD, Flash Signal, and Flash Chat protected by a shared security architecture.
- The security page emphasizes CJIS requirements, AWS GovCloud (US), AES-256 encryption, MFA, session management, least-privilege access, role-based access control, audit logging, data retention, and business continuity.
- The security whitepaper describes a security-first posture for criminal justice and sensitive environments, including Amazon Cognito, CSRF protection, bounded sessions, Vanta-supported SOC 2 work, and CJIS-aligned identity/access/encryption/logging/change-management controls.
- AWS documentation confirms that AWS GovCloud (US) is designed for government customers and partners building secure cloud workloads aligned with frameworks including CJIS.
