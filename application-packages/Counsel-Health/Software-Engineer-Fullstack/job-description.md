## About Counsel

Counsel's mission is to multiply the world's healthcare capacity. Counsel is building a fully vertical, AI-native medical group with custom AI tools for in-house physicians. Since its founding in 2023, Counsel has formed partnerships with healthcare organizations, expects to serve hundreds of thousands of patients on its platform within the next year, and has raised over $35M from a16z, Google Ventures, Pear VC, and other investors.

## About The Role

Counsel is looking for a fullstack software engineer to help define the future of AI-powered, human-led healthcare. The role is hybrid from New York City Flatiron, with a Boston office opening soon. The engineer will carry real ownership over product work, shape the engineering organization as it grows, work cross-functionally with product, clinical, and commercial teams, and interface with healthcare organizations nationwide. Engineering work spans full-stack web development, data infrastructure, mobile development, and AI/MLOps.

## What You'll Do

- Work closely with Product and Design to ship and iterate on product experiences in AI-enabled care that improve patient engagement and outcomes.
- Work closely with the ML research team to bring cutting-edge AI/LLM techniques into clinical care and ship them in production.
- Improve the physician-facing care platform and integrate AI tooling that increases physician efficiency and clinical capability.
- Build robust integrations with large enterprise clients, including EMR integrations with healthcare providers, claims data structuring with health plans, and HIE records parsing.
