## Useful Facts For Tailoring

- WelbeHealth delivers the Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly, or PACE, which coordinates medical and support services so eligible seniors can remain at home and in their communities instead of moving into nursing facilities.
- WelbeHealth describes itself as a public benefit company focused on helping seniors thrive with connection, vitality, and meaning.
- WelbeHealth's PACE model is interdisciplinary and whole-person oriented, coordinating primary and specialist care, prescriptions, rehabilitation, transportation, nutrition, social services, in-home services, and engagement programs.
- Public career-site material reports 2,500+ team members, 13+ centers, and 5,000+ participants enrolled in WelbeHealth communities.
- The posting does not state no-sponsorship, U.S.-citizen-only, permanent-resident-only, or clearance language. E-Verify/STEM OPT support remains unconfirmed.
