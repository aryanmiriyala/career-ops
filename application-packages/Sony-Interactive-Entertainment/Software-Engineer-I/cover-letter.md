Those projects gave me practice translating technical requirements into usable interfaces, maintaining clear system boundaries, and documenting enough design context for other engineers to review the work.

I would welcome the chance to bring that same practical engineering approach to Sony Interactive Entertainment through careful TypeScript/JavaScript implementation, secure full-stack architecture, and data-aware judgment for systems that support PlayStation-scale product decisions.
