Dear HiringCafe Team,

I am applying for the Founding Full-stack / Product Engineer role because the problem feels very real to me. My closest match is a project I built around the same problem space. Across those projects, I have had to move between UI, backend routes, data models, auth, validation, and practical product tradeoffs. What stands out about HiringCafe is the combination of product urgency and real user impact. A founding engineering role is a stretch in responsibility, but it is the kind of environment where I would want to be close to users, ship carefully, and own work end to end instead of only touching a narrow slice of the stack.
