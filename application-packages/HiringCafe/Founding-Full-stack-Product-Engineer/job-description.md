HiringCafe says job discovery is broken and that Indeed and LinkedIn want to keep it that way. The company is building a much better job search engine that is fast, comprehensive, honest, and actually useful. HiringCafe indexes millions of jobs, removes noise, ranks what matters, and helps people find real opportunities without dark patterns, ads, or pay-to-win placement.

HiringCafe says it does not believe in hype and instead believes in shipping product and listening obsessively to users. In 12 months, it grew from 0 to 1 million+ monthly active users, so shipped work goes directly to real people.

## What HiringCafe Is Building

- AI agents that help people discover opportunities they would not have found on their own
- A two-sided marketplace connecting every job on earth to the best candidate
- Scalable infrastructure that transforms fragmented web data into a clean, searchable platform
- A product where engineering, data, and design decisions directly shape how people find their next chapter
