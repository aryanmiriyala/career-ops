## Why are you interested in joining HiringCafe?

I honestly don't enjoy job hunting. I'd rather spend that time building software products that are useful, which is why I ended up making my own Career Ops tool. It uses an AI agent to help run parts of the application-package workflow, while I still keep the source material, validation gates, and final decisions under my control. Building it made me realize how much of job search is just messy data, stale postings, bad filtering, and repetitive work that should be handled better.

HiringCafe stood out to me because it is attacking that exact problem in a serious way. I actually found it while searching for better job boards to use, and someone on Reddit recommended it. That felt like a good sign because it came from a real job seeker, not an ad. The product feels honest and practical, and I like that the team is focused on making job discovery genuinely better instead of adding more noise to an already frustrating process.

## How did you hear about HiringCafe?

I found HiringCafe while looking for better job boards to use in my own job search. Someone on Reddit recommended it, so I checked it out and it immediately stood out because it felt cleaner and more useful than the usual job boards.
