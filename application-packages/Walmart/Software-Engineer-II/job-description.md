## Position Summary

### Role Summary
As a Software Engineer II within the Command & Control Center (CCC), you will help ensure the availability, reliability, and performance of Walmart's critical omnichannel eCommerce platforms. You will proactively monitor production environments, detect and triage incidents, analyze application and infrastructure health, execute mitigation and recovery procedures, and collaborate with engineering teams to rapidly restore customer-facing services. You will also engineer monitoring, alerting, automation, API integrations, and operational tooling using modern observability, cloud, container, and software-development technologies. The role provides hands-on experience supporting large-scale distributed systems while continuously improving detection, incident response, automation, and operational resilience.

### About the Team
The Command & Control Center (CCC) is a 24x7 Site Reliability Operations team ensuring the availability, reliability, and performance of Walmart's critical omnichannel eCommerce platforms. The team proactively monitors production environments, detects, triage and mitigate incidents, assesses business impact, and coordinates rapid service recovery. CCC supports cloud-native and distributed environments across Linux/Unix, Azure, Google Cloud Platform, Kubernetes, microservices, networking, and modern observability technologies. Beyond incident response, the team enhances monitoring, alerting, API integrations, operational tooling, automation, and self-healing capabilities while partnering with SRE, DevOps, and engineering teams to improve operational readiness, reduce recovery time, and strengthen platform resilience.
