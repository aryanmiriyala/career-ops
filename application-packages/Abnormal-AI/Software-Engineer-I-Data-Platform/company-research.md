## Useful Company/Role Signals

- Abnormal positions itself as an AI-native cybersecurity company focused on behavioral AI for email, identity, AI, and insider-threat protection.
- The pasted role is on Platform & Infrastructure and centers on the Data Platform team, not application feature work alone.
- The role language repeatedly emphasizes fast, reliable, and secure access to massive datasets, scalable storage systems, batch and stream data processing, infrastructure automation, and developer/operator experience.
- The strongest submitted-material angle should be data-platform software engineering: Python, PySpark, AWS Glue, S3, IAM, PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, Kafka/Spark exposure, validation, reliability, and secure data movement.
- Abnormal's careers pages and pasted posting include an export-compliance notice. This is an internal eligibility follow-up item, but it is not submitted-material content.

## Package Implications

- Lead resume proof with AAIS production data engineering because it is the strongest direct match to massive data, cloud ETL, PostgreSQL, AWS, reliability, and automation.
- Keep SmartSolve near the top because it supports developer/operator tooling, PostgreSQL, SSO/auth middleware, Docker devcontainers, and AI-assisted development.
- Use HealthTrend for Kafka/Spark batch and streaming adjacency.
- Use Fix-It-Flow for DynamoDB, typed services, stateful AI workflows, and GenAI operator/developer experience.
- Avoid unsupported Abnormal stack terms such as Go, Databricks, OpenSearch/Elasticsearch, Redis, RocksDB, Airflow, and DBT in the resume and cover letter.
