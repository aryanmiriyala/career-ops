## Job Description

I'm hiring a full-time full-stack developer to help build Nenbase.

Remote · $50–$100 USD per hour · Potential equity

About three months ago, my co-founder and I started building Nenbase—a CRM, tracking, and automation platform for coaching businesses and teams that sell through sales calls.

Today, we have:
→ $13K in monthly recurring revenue
→ 37 active paying subaccounts
→ 150 active users

We started with five paying beta customers. Growth since then has come through referrals—before a public marketing launch, paid advertising, or outbound campaigns.

Now we're looking for our first full-time developer to help us take the product further.

### Why we're building this

Before Nenbase, I helped coaches generate millions in sales and built the backend systems behind their businesses.

That meant connecting tools like Zapier, Google Sheets, GoHighLevel, Typeform, and Calendly—then repeating the process for every new client.

Nenbase brings those workflows into one place, with AI helping customers set up their systems, automations, and tracking without needing technical experience.

I've stepped away from my previous businesses to focus on this full-time.

Along the way, I built an audience of 11,000 Instagram followers and 4,000 YouTube subscribers, alongside relationships with coaches and operators who know and trust my work.

We have direct access to our customers, firsthand experience with the problem, and an existing network to reach as we prepare to launch publicly.

### What you'll own

You'll work directly with me and my co-founder, taking ownership of the engineering quality behind a product customers already pay for.

We're hands-on founders who build extensively with Claude and Codex. We want someone who can move quickly with us, challenge our decisions when needed, and turn new features into reliable production software.

Your work will include:
• Reviewing, improving, testing, and shipping features
• Owning safe releases, troubleshooting, and production reliability
• Strengthening security, customer permissions, and database performance
• Building integrations for email, SMS, calling, Meta, Twilio, and other services
• Handling technical integration requirements and provider approval processes
• Helping us prioritize engineering work as the product grows

### Our current setup

Supabase, Vercel, and GitHub, with an AI-assisted development workflow.

We may evaluate AWS as we scale, but a migration isn't the immediate assignment. The priority is improving and extending the live product.

### Who we're looking for

You've personally built, shipped, and maintained SaaS products. You're comfortable working across frontend, backend, databases, authentication, permissions, and APIs.

You use AI coding tools to move faster, while understanding the code well enough to catch mistakes and take responsibility for what goes live.

You communicate clearly, raise issues early, and follow through.

Experience building CRMs, communications platforms, or software for coaching businesses is a strong bonus.

This is an intensive early-stage role: approximately 60–70+ hours per week, with consistent overlap with US Pacific working hours.

We're ambitious, deeply involved in the product, and in frequent contact with customers. You'll have direct access to both founders, fast decisions, and meaningful influence over how Nenbase develops.

We're looking for someone who wants to take ownership and build with us long-term.
