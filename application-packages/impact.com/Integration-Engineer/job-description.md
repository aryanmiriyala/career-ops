## Company Context

impact.com is a commerce partnership marketing platform that helps brands discover, manage, and scale partnerships across affiliates, influencers, content publishers, brand ambassadors, and customer advocates. The platform includes Performance, Creator, and Advocate products. The posting states that over 5,000 global brands, including Walmart, Uber, Shopify, Lenovo, L'Oreal, and Fanatics, rely on impact.com to power more than 350,000 partnerships.

## Role Summary

The Integration Engineer learns impact.com's technology and assists with proposing and implementing technical solutions for clients on the company's SaaS platform. The role works with Onboarding, Client Success, and internal teams, interacts with business and technical client stakeholders, answers integration-process and best-practice questions, and helps clients go live efficiently.

## Responsibilities

- Stay current with impact.com's platform capabilities, product enhancements, and relevant industry trends.
- Support the design and integration of technical solutions for new and existing clients.
- Lead technical discovery and solution discussions to understand client requirements and propose optimal solutions based on client objectives.
- Contribute to technical integration documentation.
- Partner with Onboarding to deliver a seamless, efficient, high-quality client onboarding experience.
- Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues encountered during client onboarding.
