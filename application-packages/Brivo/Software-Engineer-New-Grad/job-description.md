# Software Engineer - NEW GRAD

Brivo is hiring Software Engineers to join our Engineering organization across two high-impact teams: Video (our global cloud video surveillance platform) and Access (the backend and cloud services powering Brivo's physical security platform). This posting is your gateway to either team — during the interview process, we'll work with you to find the best fit based on your background, interests, and our current team needs.

This role is built for recent college graduates who are curious, eager to learn, and ready to grow into professional software engineers with the support of experienced mentors. You'll be treated as a full member of the team from day one: attending standups, participating in code reviews, and owning real deliverables that reach customers globally.

## Key Responsibilities

Build with cutting-edge AI: Leverage the latest AI models to rapidly prototype, develop, and iterate on next-generation security products, APIs, and MCP servers.

Deploy at startup speed: Collaborate within small, agile product teams alongside other top-notch product engineers to test new ideas and push code to production on a weekly—or even daily—basis.

Scale your impact: Capitalize on the powerful, newly integrated Brivo and Eagle Eye platform to build everything from bespoke applications for boutique clients to rock-solid, enterprise-grade security suites.

Bring fresh, AI-native innovation: Apply your deep curiosity to maximize our platform's potential. No prior industry experience is required—just an unstoppable drive to learn, iterate, and create.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field
- Working knowledge of at least one of: Python, Java, or Node.js/TypeScript (coursework, internships, or personal projects all count)
- Basic understanding of databases and SQL
- Comfort using Linux and running shell commands
- Familiarity with Git or another version control system
- Strong curiosity, problem-solving skills, and attention to detail

## Nice to Have

- Intro-level knowledge of networking concepts (IP, ports, protocols, APIs)
- Experience with cloud platforms, especially AWS
- Exposure to Docker or containerized environments
- Familiarity with REST APIs and HTTP fundamentals
- Experience writing automated tests (e.g., Playwright)
- Exposure to authentication/authorization concepts (OAuth 2.0, OIDC, Auth0)

## About Brivo

Brivo and Eagle Eye Networks have come together to create a category-defining, cloud-first platform at the intersection of AI and the physical world. Our mission is simple and ambitious: to make physical spaces safer, smarter, and more autonomous—from single-site businesses to global enterprises.

As the unified global leaders in cloud-based access control and video surveillance, we provide a comprehensive digital foundation for the built environment. By combining Brivo's pioneering building access platform with Eagle Eye's AI-powered video management system (VMS), we have created a system of intelligence that protects over 600 million square feet across 90+ countries. Every door, camera, and credential in our ecosystem acts as a real-time sensor, leveraging AI to prevent incidents, improve operations, and enable entirely new workflows across sectors, including commercial real estate, retail, healthcare, education, and critical infrastructure.

With a global footprint spanning headquarters in Bethesda, Maryland, and Austin, Texas, and international offices in Amsterdam, London, Bangalore, and Tokyo, we are leveraging our open APIs and cyber-secure cloud infrastructure to reshape the future of physical security. Join us as we build the world's most robust platform for video intelligence and smart space automation. Learn more at www.brivo.com.
