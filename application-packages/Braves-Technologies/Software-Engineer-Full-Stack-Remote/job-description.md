Braves describes its culture as focused on high performance, high delivery, diverse thinking, collaboration at all levels, learning throughout the organization, ownership, and work-life balance.

## About The Role

The role works directly on the core product: campaign creation, personalization pipelines, delivery tracking, analytics, and integrations with modern GTM tools. Braves is looking for a full-stack engineer with 2-5 years of experience who cares about shipping fast, building clean systems, and designing product experiences that feel magical.

## What You'll Do

- Build and ship full-stack product features using React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Node.
- Design and implement APIs that power campaign creation, personalization, and delivery tracking.
- Work on systems that generate, render, and queue physical mail dynamically.
- Build internal tooling and dashboards for campaign analytics and operations.
- Integrate with CRMs, data providers, and outbound tooling such as Salesforce and HubSpot.
- Optimize performance and reliability across the stack.
- Collaborate closely with design and GTM to ship fast iterations.
- Communicate technical tradeoffs clearly and concisely.

## What Braves Is Looking For

- 2-5 years of experience building modern production web apps.
- Strong skills in React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Node.js.
- Experience working across the stack, frontend and backend.
- Solid understanding of APIs, databases, Postgres preferred, and system design.
- Ability to move quickly and ship in a fast-paced startup environment.
- Strong product sense, thinking about user experience and not only code.
- Comfort owning features end-to-end.
- Ability to leverage AI tools to build faster and better.
- Clear written and verbal communication in English.

## Nice To Have

- Experience with rendering pipelines, PDF generation, templating, etc.
- Experience with queues, background jobs, and event-driven systems.
- Experience integrating with CRMs or GTM tooling.
- Experience working on growth or outbound-focused products and startup experience.
