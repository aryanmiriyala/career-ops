## Useful Company Context

- Braves presents itself as a company that builds dedicated engineering teams in India for global clients, including clients in the US, EU, and Australia.
- The specific full-stack role is tied to a core product involving campaign creation, personalization pipelines, delivery tracking, analytics, and GTM integrations.
- The company emphasizes high delivery, ownership, collaboration, communication, and learning.
- Braves' site highlights distributed-team operations, cultural alignment, managed operations, and small-team growth. That makes end-to-end ownership, clear technical tradeoff communication, and remote collaboration useful cover-letter themes.

- Lead with product-minded full-stack engineering rather than generic software ability.
- Use exact stack language where truthful: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, APIs, Postgres/PostgreSQL, databases, internal tooling, dashboards, AI tools, PDF generation, and event-driven systems.
- Keep CRM, GTM tooling, physical-mail queues, and outbound-focused product language in tailoring notes as gaps unless Aryan can confirm direct work.
- Cover letter should connect Braves' high-delivery distributed-team model to Aryan's history of owning internal tools, secure workflows, AI-assisted development, and API/data workflow projects.
