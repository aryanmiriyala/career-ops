# Application Questions - Lightfield Software Engineer Infrastructure Early Career

## How did you hear about Lightfield?

I found the role through Jobright, which led me to Lightfield's Ashby posting. After that I read more about the product and the team because the role sounded unusually close to the kind of work I have been targeting.

## Why are you excited about Lightfield and this role?

I like that Lightfield is trying to make CRM feel less like manual data entry and more like a living record of actual customer interactions. That problem feels concrete to me because I have worked on internal workflow tools, data models, and AI-backed products where the hard part was not just the interface or the model call. It was making the surrounding system reliable enough for people to trust. At SmartSolve I built Next.js and PostgreSQL internal tooling, and at AAIS I worked on AWS data infrastructure over large insurance datasets. This role feels like a chance to keep building that kind of product infrastructure in an applied AI setting.
