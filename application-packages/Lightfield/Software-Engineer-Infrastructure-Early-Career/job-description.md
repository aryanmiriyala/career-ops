## About Lightfield

Lightfield is an AI-native CRM that assembles itself from your email, calendar, and meetings. It captures every interaction and turns it into organized context: accounts, tasks, follow-ups, and insights, so nothing slips through the cracks.

Lightfield is rethinking CRM from first principles. Instead of forcing teams to maintain rigid systems, Lightfield learns from how companies actually work, adapting, automating, and surfacing the insight that drives growth. The company is building a CRM platform that is fast, intelligent, and genuinely helpful.

Lightfield is backed by Greylock, Lightspeed, and Coatue, and its founders previously built Tome, a generative AI presentation product used by over 25 million people. Before Lightfield, the team worked on Llama, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Pinterest, Google, and Salesforce.

## About The Role

Lightfield is looking for a creative and versatile engineer eager to tackle the challenge of architecting, building, and scaling the core infrastructure and systems powering Lightfield's AI-driven CRM.

Engineers take ownership of projects from inception to impact, collaborate across functions, and push the envelope on what is possible in applied AI and data systems. The role is critical to designing and building the data models and infrastructure that support a next-generation CRM, ensuring the system is performant, scalable, and resilient.
