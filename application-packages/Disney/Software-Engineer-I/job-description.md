## Job Summary

Technology is at the heart of Disney's past, present, and future. Disney Entertainment and ESPN Product & Technology is a global organization of engineers, product developers, designers, technologists, data scientists, and more, all working to build and advance the technological backbone for Disney's media business globally.

The team marries technology with creativity to build world-class products, enhance storytelling, and drive velocity, innovation, and scalability for Disney's businesses. The organization works across Disney's media portfolio to advance the technological foundation and consumer media touch points serving millions of people around the world.

Content Platforms & Operations is responsible for the development and ongoing advancement of the technical and operational functions driving the worldwide distribution and monetization of Disney's linear networks and theatrical content. This includes Disney's media supply chain and storage, network and theatrical distribution operations, media localization, network transmission and origination, and more.

The Rights & Accounting fleet oversees products and optimizes workflows that enable TWDC to monetize content including content rights and avails management, content sales and content protection, content accounting, and talent payments.

As a Software Engineer, this role works across Rights Management supply chain applications, building and supporting capabilities, and taking ownership of technical design and implementation of medium-sized projects and features.

The role focuses on systems that govern and track the rights and availability of TWDC media for use in first-party products such as Disney+ and third-party digital media sales. The engineer collaborates with multiple internal and external teams to enable robust data integration capabilities.
