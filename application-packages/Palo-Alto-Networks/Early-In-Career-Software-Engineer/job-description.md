## The Team

Engineering - The Engineering team is at the core of Palo Alto Networks products and connected services. Engineers work on technologies that change how people consume security and work in the digital age. The team is positioned around collaboration and solving challenging cybersecurity threats.

## Job Summary

The role joins a Research and Development team with the opportunity to make a global impact by helping prevent successful cyber attacks. Projects may involve hands-on work on products from Strata, Prisma, and Cortex organizations.

## Key Responsibilities

- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to design, develop, and deploy software solutions with agility and rapid iteration.
- Use and experiment with AI-assisted development tools such as Cursor, Claude Code, and internal agents to boost productivity and improve code quality.
- Help define and evolve engineering practices that use AI for code generation, testing, documentation, refactoring, and operations.
- Write clean, maintainable code and participate in peer reviews and team design discussions.
- Contribute to automation of development, deployment, and monitoring processes as part of a DevOps culture.
- Continuously learn and apply new AI technologies, frameworks, and techniques that can enhance developer workflows.
- Participate in team retrospectives and help drive improvements in team velocity and product quality.

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree earned between December 2024 and May/June 2025 with at least 1 year of professional experience, full-time experience only.
- Master's degree earned between December 2025 and May/June 2026 with at least 1 year of professional experience prior to Master's.
- Degree focus must be Machine Learning, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, or something similar.
- Strong programming skills in either Python, Java, or C/C++.
- Familiarity with networking fundamentals for Embedded and Distributed Systems positions.
- Understanding of the fundamentals of Computer Science.
- Strong initiative and ability to work independently with limited direction.
- Ability to work cross-functionally with engineers, researchers, and Product Managers.
- Excellent communication skills and an interest in cybersecurity.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Exposure to large language models or AI/ML APIs, even through personal projects.
- Familiarity with cloud environments such as GCP, AWS, or Azure and containerized development such as Docker or Kubernetes.
- Experience with modern developer tools such as CI/CD pipelines, observability platforms, and infrastructure-as-code.
