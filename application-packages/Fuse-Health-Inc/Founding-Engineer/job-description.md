## About the Job

Fuse Health is building the infrastructure layer for telehealth. The company describes itself as a launched startup on a mission to change how providers and patients connect through scalable, secure technology. Version one of the product is live, and the company is looking for an engineer to own it, harden it, and build what comes next.

The posting frames the role as the technical engine of the company, not a ticket-taking role. The engineer is expected to lead themselves, make architectural calls, ship quickly, and care about quality. The posting specifically calls out people who have founded something before or who build fully functional projects outside work.

## What You'll Do

- Take full ownership of the product: backend, frontend, infrastructure, and everything in between.
- Make architectural decisions that set the foundation for scale, security, and reliability.
- Identify what needs to be built, improved, or rearchitected and go do it.
- Optimize system performance, squash hard bugs, and keep the platform rock-solid.
- Collaborate directly with the founder to align technical execution with business goals.
- Lead code quality through reviews, testing, and engineering best practices.
- Wear every hat that needs wearing in an early-stage startup.

## What They're Looking For

- A true full-stack engineer comfortable across frontend, backend, APIs, databases, cloud infrastructure, and DevOps.
- Someone who has led themselves before, such as a previous founder, solo builder, or engineer who thrives without a manager telling them what to do next.
- A track record of shipping real, working products, whether startups, side projects, or open-source work.
- Deep backend expertise: data modeling, system design, API architecture, and cloud platforms.
- Strong instincts for when to move fast and when to invest the time to do it right.
- Excellent communication skills and ability to explain technical decisions clearly.
- Experience with or genuine interest in telehealth or healthcare technology as a major plus.
- Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent hands-on experience.

## Role Demands

- Founding engineer position at a just-launched company.
- 12+ hour days when the work requires it.
- Ambiguity, context switching, and building across the entire stack in a single afternoon.
- Large ownership, direct impact, and shaping product and company from the ground floor.
