## Useful Signals

- Primitive Labs describes its mission as making human behavior a first-class primitive of software development.
- The company frames its work around behavioral intelligence for AI-native software, arguing that as software moves faster from idea to production, understanding what to build and how people respond becomes the bottleneck.
- The launch blog says Primitive Labs is building simulation architectures, memory systems, and agent interfaces for behavioral intelligence, with research centered on formal state modeling, computational cognitive science, and continual learning.
- The founding team comes from Amazon Artificial General Intelligence and Amazon Web Services.
- The careers page emphasizes a small builder culture where the whole team pushes code and contributes to research, with real ownership and direct feedback.
